Allbox

Allbox is a small personal mail app for Windows. It shows several Gmail accounts in one window, so their owner can read, write, archive and delete mail in one place.

It is a private project, built for its developer's own accounts. It is not offered to the public and cannot be downloaded here.

Why it asks for Gmail access

Allbox needs access to Gmail to do its one job: showing and managing the mail of the signed-in account. It asks for the gmail.modify permission only, which does not allow permanent deletion of mail.

Mail stays on the user's own PC and goes nowhere except Google. See the privacy policy and the terms of service.
